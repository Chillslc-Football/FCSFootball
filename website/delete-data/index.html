FCS Pulse Data Deletion

FCS Pulse is a free FCS football scores and media app. You may request deletion of personal data associated with your use of FCS Pulse.

To request deletion, email privacy@fcspulse.com.

Please include enough information for us to identify the data you want deleted. Depending on how you used the app, applicable data may include:

  • Email address or name voluntarily submitted through feedback or media suggestion forms
  • Feedback or media suggestion content
  • Device identifier and associated notification preferences, favorites, or followed games when that information can reasonably be identified

FCS Pulse will delete applicable personal data associated with a valid request.

Limited information may be retained when reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal obligations, or maintaining records of completed deletion requests.
